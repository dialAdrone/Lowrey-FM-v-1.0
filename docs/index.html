The real firmware, running in your browser

Install on your FM-1 GitHub
MASTER
Play: click the keys, or use your computer keyboard: the home row A S D F G H J K L ; ' plays the white keys from F, the row above (W E R Y U O P [) the black ones. Knobs: drag up/down or scroll over them. Buttons can be held. A MIDI keyboard works too in Chrome or Edge. The 8 lower buttons switch the voices on the screen's cards. Settings are kept in this browser.
